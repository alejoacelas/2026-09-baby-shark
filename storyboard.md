# Storyboard

The shark family is introduced one by one while Steve, a small orange fish who
"is not in this song", keeps trying to get into the family photo. When the
family goes hunting, the food turns out to be Steve. Steve's mom chases them
off, and they hide in a cave that turns out to be Steve's house. Then the shot
zooms out to a fishbowl, then to a bored teenager's computer, and the teenager
doesn't save the drawing. The unsaved sharks realise they don't exist, and
Steve's mom calls him in for dinner.

Running threads: the family photo frame gets redrawn bigger each time (the
old frames are badly erased); Steve asks to be included and ends up crossed
out with "(adopted)"; mothers keep asking about food ("have you eaten",
"you look thin", "dinner").

Cut times are the start of each sung line, measured on the vocal stem (see
`src/timing/`). Every line gets its own drawing; most are the previous
drawing with one thing added.
