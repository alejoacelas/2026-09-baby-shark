# Style hints

These come from a 2010 fan video for "Mi agüita amarilla", drawn in Windows 7 MS Paint
with a mouse and cut into a slideshow. They're for inspiration, not rules.

- **Examples in `frames/`:** `stick-figure.png` (the narrator), `river-cows.png`
  (freehand animals, handwritten labels, airbrushed pee in the water),
  `pipe-labels.png` (handwritten and typed labels side by side, block arrows),
  `cloud-city.png` (shape-tool buildings, flat fills, spray-can cloud).
- **Colours:** Paint's default swatches, mostly white backgrounds with light
  turquoise (#99D9EA) water and sky, yellow (#FFF200) for the pee, lime, brown,
  gray. No gradients or shading.
- **Lines:** a thick round black brush pushed by a mouse: wobbly, overshooting,
  joints that don't meet. Props that should be round or boxy (toilet, sun,
  buildings) use Paint's perfect ellipse and rectangle tools, and the contrast
  with the shaky freehand is part of the charm.
- **Fills:** flat bucket fills that leave white gaps at the outline edges. Soft
  things (clouds, vapour, pee diluted in water) are spray-can speckles.
- **Text:** mostly shaky handwritten capitals, often with an arrow to the thing
  labelled; a few labels in Paint's plain typed font, inconsistently.
- **Pacing:** hard cuts, a new drawing for roughly every noun in the lyrics. One
  base scene gets reused with only the speech bubble changed.
